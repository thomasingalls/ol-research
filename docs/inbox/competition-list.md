# Competition and adjacent tools

Updated 2026-09-24. One row per service, with every documented area of
overlap marked independently. Read across to see a service's span and down
to find alternatives for a particular capability. Rows are alphabetical.

This is a working inventory of advertised capabilities, not a product
quality ranking or an exhaustive feature audit. Infrastructure and access
services may also be suppliers or integration partners.

## What the columns mean

| Column | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Find | Search or recommend literature beyond a user's saved library. |
| Trace | Follow citations, paper similarity, or concept relationships; includes exposed graph data. |
| Check | Resolve or repair references, or inspect citation context and evidence status. |
| AI read | Summarize, extract, compare, or synthesize paper content with AI. |
| Alerts | Monitor new research through notifications, saved searches, or feeds. |
| Library | Save and organize papers, collections, PDFs, or notes. |
| Cite | Generate formatted citations or bibliographies, or insert them while writing. |
| Access | Locate, retrieve, deliver, or license full-text articles; uploads and retrieved snippets alone do not count. |
| API/data | Provide a documented API, MCP service, or bulk dataset; ordinary citation exports do not count. |
| Metrics | Provide aggregate research analytics, bibliometrics, or research-impact indicators. |
| Share | Share research collections or libraries, or support researcher collaboration. |

A mark records overlap, not equivalence. A one-sentence summary and a
multi-paper review both fall under AI reading; citation links and visual
concept maps both fall under tracing. The notes retain those distinctions.
Reference checking is separate from generating a bibliography, and
programmatic access is separate from owning a corpus.

## Scope and evidence

- **Academia.edu.** Research sharing and paper discovery.
- **Ai2 Asta.** Decomposes research questions, searches, follows citations, evaluates relevance, and synthesizes literature. Its [Scientific Corpus MCP](https://allenai.org/asta/resources/mcp) exposes paper search, citation retrieval, metadata lookup, and passage search for other agents.
- **AnswerThis.** Literature reviews, source/claim checking, libraries, writing with formatted citations, and shared workspaces. These are advertised capabilities; its accuracy and compliance claims have not been evaluated here.
- **Causaly.** Biomedical relationships and enterprise research workflows, with a Bio Graph API. Its [Scientific Information Retrieval System](https://www.causaly.com/ai-platform/scientific-rag) combines similarity retrieval with graph-based search and ranking over biomedical concepts and relationships.
- **CCC / RightFind.** The row covers the RightFind suite's discovery, shared libraries, access, and licensing. [RightFind Insight](https://www.copyright.com/solutions-rightfind-insight/) adds semantic search and concept navigation.
- **ChatPDF.** Document Q&A, summaries, multi-file folders, and sharing, plus a [PDF extraction/chat API](https://www.chatpdf.com/docs/api/backend). [AI Research](https://www.chatpdf.com/scholar) adds literature search, abstract-based synthesis, and full-paper chat for open-access papers. Links back to source passages do not by themselves establish reference checking or bibliography generation. Its homepage advertises 10M+ users and 1M+ questions answered daily; these are provider-reported adoption figures, not independently verified active-user or revenue measurements.
- **Connected Papers.** Visual exploration of related papers.
- **Consensus.** The feature catalogue includes citation graphs, shared collections, bibliography creation, and an API alongside search and synthesis.
- **CORE.** Aggregated research metadata and open-access full text, available through search and machine access.
- **Crossref.** DOI metadata, deposited references, and reference matching; graph overlap is through the reference data. [Retrieval documentation](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/) covers APIs and bulk access.
- **DeepDyve.** LitHub combines search, AI reading, reference management, Word citation tools, shared storage, and full-text access.
- **Dimensions.** Linked research information, discovery, visualization, and AI. [API access](https://docs.dimensions.ai/dsl/api.html) depends on the subscribed product and its permitted uses.
- **Edison Literature.** PaperQA3 reads scientific text, figures, and tables, checks provenance/retraction status, and is available through an API. The [platform documentation](https://docs.edisonscientific.com/) describes citation-graph-aware synthesis; its separate Precedent agent searches for earlier work on an idea. Hosted capabilities should not be assumed identical to the open-source PaperQA package.
- **Elicit.** Search, evidence extraction, synthesis, alerts, shared libraries, and API/MCP access are listed on the product site.
- **EndNote.** Reference management, reference updates, full-text retrieval, and AI reading. Citation following uses [Web of Science integration](https://endnote.com/blog/introducing-endnote-2025-ai-powered-reference-management/) and requires the relevant subscription. [Library sharing](https://endnote.com/) is part of the product.
- **GetFTR.** Entitlement-aware linking from discovery tools to full text.
- **Google Scholar.** Search, citing/related papers, alerts, saved libraries, formatted citations, and links to accessible copies.
- **Iris.ai / RSpace.** Research discovery, concept/topic analysis, and full-text chat. [RSpace 1.3](https://iris.ai/blog/announcement-new-features-in-r-space-1-3) documents OpenAlex integration, research monitoring, saved datasets, and collaborative versioning. Its [Analyze tool](https://iris.ai/blog/tech-deep-dive-topic-modelling-with-the-analyze-tool) extracts concepts for filtering; this does not establish autonomous citation traversal.
- **The Lens.** Scholarly and patent discovery, citation links, collections, and analytics; [API documentation](https://docs.api.lens.org/) covers corpus access.
- **Litmaps.** Citation maps and saved research collections, monitoring, and shared maps; also integrates with Zotero.
- **Mendeley.** PDF organization and annotation, AI reading, and shared group libraries. [Mendeley Cite](https://www.mendeley.com/reference-management/mendeley-cite) handles citations in Word.
- **Mistral / Vibe + Studio.** Vibe (formerly Le Chat) offers deep research and shared workspaces. [Agentic Search](https://mistral.ai/news/agentic-search/) provides iterative document search/reading through Search Toolkit and Libraries. Scholarly discovery is marked as an integration through Wiley Scholar Gateway. This is a general research/deployment platform; specific FDE engagements and scholarly citation-graph capabilities remain unestablished here.
- **Open Knowledge Maps.** Topic maps and concept-based research overviews.
- **OpenAIRE Graph.** Linked research information, graph exploration, datasets, and APIs.
- **OpenAlex.** Scholarly metadata and research relationships; [documentation](https://help.openalex.org/) covers data and API access.
- **OpenCitations.** Open bibliographic and citation lookup and data infrastructure.
- **Paperpile.** [Search and collection](https://paperpile.com/features/collect-papers/) use external scholarly databases and retrieve PDFs. Its [AI integration](https://paperpile.com/blog/pdf-ai-assistant/) sends PDFs and prompts to external assistants; marked as an integration, not an in-product synthesis engine.
- **Papers / ReadCube.** Search, AI reading and extraction, monitoring, shared libraries, SmartCite, and document delivery; features span multiple plans.
- **Papers with Code (.co).** Machine-learning papers, methods, benchmarks, datasets, and implementations. This entry refers specifically to the supplied .co site.
- **PubMed.** Biomedical discovery, related/citing articles, citation matching, alerts/RSS, My NCBI collections, citations, full-text links, and programmatic retrieval. Full text is linked from other providers.
- **Research Solutions / Article Galaxy.** Discovery and article acquisition, with [article summarization](https://www.researchsolutions.com/article-galaxy-ai-integration-faq) and [APIs/MCPs](https://www.researchsolutions.com/api-and-mcp). This row covers Article Galaxy; scite has its own row.
- **ResearchGate.** Research sharing, researcher networks, and collaboration.
- **ResearchRabbit.** Recommendations, paper/author/concept maps, and organization of research topics and collections.
- **scienceOS.** Its documented agent combines search, citation-network expansion, full-text PDF reading, libraries, and shared projects. A worked example checks manuscript claims against cited papers. The PDF tool can also read publicly available open-access papers.
- **SciSpace.** Literature search and reviews, PDF chat, citation generation, and a listed API offering.
- **scite.** Citation contexts and supporting/contrasting evidence, synthesis, collections, monitoring, and APIs/MCP. Access through library holdings is an integration.
- **Scopus.** Citation indexing, discovery, and research analytics. [Scopus with AI](https://www.elsevier.com/products/scopus/scopus-ai) adds summaries and research assistance; [Scopus APIs](https://dev.elsevier.com/sc_apis.html) expose abstracts and citation data.
- **Semantic Scholar.** Paper and citation discovery, libraries, and [research-feed alerts](https://webflow.semanticscholar.org/faq/research-feed-alert). [TLDR summaries](https://webflow.semanticscholar.org/product/tldr) explain the AI mark; this does not imply a full synthesis agent. Asta has its own row.
- **Stork.** Alerts, saved papers, citation networks, ChatPaper/ChatScholar, and True Cite reference finding and verification. Its Big Analysis tool also analyzes collections of papers.
- **Third Iron / LibKey.** Routes readers to available full text through institutional and open-access holdings.
- **Undermind.** Iterative search, citation following, monitoring, shared workspaces, and agent connections. Its [MCP tool catalogue](https://www.undermind.ai/mcp) also documents fast paper/citation search, bulk title/DOI/bibliography resolution, parallel PDF reading, and automatic retrieval of open-access PDFs. Paywalled PDFs can be read after upload. This establishes overlap with reference resolution and full-text access as well as discovery and synthesis.
- **Unpaywall.** Open-access locations and article access metadata through its [API and data products](https://unpaywall.org/products/).
- **Valyu.** Scientific retrieval through REST, SDK, and MCP, including full text where licensing permits. Search, Answer, and DeepResearch span retrieval through multi-step synthesis across literature and specialist databases. Citation-graph traversal and bibliography repair are not established by the reviewed documentation.
- **Web of Science.** Citation indexing and analytics; also [Research Assistant](https://clarivate.com/academia-government/blog/advancing-research-with-ai-new-features-in-the-web-of-science-research-assistant/), [alerts](https://webofscience.help.clarivate.com/en-us/Content/saved-searches-alerts.html), and [APIs](https://developer.clarivate.com/apis).
- **Wiley / Scholar Gateway.** Searches Wiley journal full text and returns passages with bibliographic metadata and DOIs inside connected assistants, including Mistral Vibe. [MCP/API distribution](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/solutions-partnerships/ai-solutions/) overlaps the proposed agent-facing retrieval layer. Passage retrieval does not establish complete-article delivery, hence the unmarked Access cell.
- **Zotero.** Reference and PDF management, reading and annotation, feeds, writing integrations, and group libraries. Its [Web API](https://www.zotero.org/support/dev/web_api/v3/) accesses user/group libraries; it is not a corpus-search API.

## Undermind overlap — 2026-09-24

The user identified Undermind as directly overlapping the intended product.
Its [published tools](https://www.undermind.ai/mcp) support the research loop
in these notes: find candidates, follow citations, read papers in parallel,
resolve references, synthesize, and retain a workspace for further work.
This also reaches the proposed agent-facing service, beyond the researcher
interface. Its [GSK case study](https://www.undermind.ai/case-studies/gsk)
describes programmatic use within GSK's AI research platform and tracing
the citation and temporal evolution of evidence; it reports over 1,000 GSK
scientists using the product. These are vendor-published claims, not a
hands-on assessment here.

The more specific proposed [concept graph](future-ambitions.md#66-build-then-traverse)
has typed credit/identity relationships, historical renamings, independent
discoveries, and multiple traversals over a retained graph. Whether
Undermind can deliver that workflow remains untested. The reviewed pages
do not establish a gap. Likewise, source-disagreement preservation,
reproducibility, cost, latency, and deployment control are dimensions to
compare, not demonstrated competitive advantages. The earlier claim in
[product shapes](product-shapes.md) that nobody at the frontier does the
tracer was too strong.

The focused [Undermind alternatives comparison](undermind-alternatives.md)
covers Asta, Edison, scienceOS, Valyu, and Causaly, plus Mistral's public
research and deployment offerings. It distinguishes documented mechanisms
from capabilities that still need hands-on comparison.
